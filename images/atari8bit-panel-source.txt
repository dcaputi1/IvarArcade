Original composition: white background, headings, divider, and controller labels added;
photographs converted from JPEG to a single PNG using Windows System.Drawing.
Edited 2026-11-02 using an AI image editor to change the background to light
brown/tan, requesting preservation of the controllers, layout, and labels.
The edited output may differ from the original photographic composite.
Reformatted to 4:3 with matching tan padding above and below; content scaled
proportionally without cropping or stretching the controllers.
No claim is made that the source photographs were PNG files.
